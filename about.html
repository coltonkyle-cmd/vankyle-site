About

Colton Kyle

Real estate developer in Shreveport, Louisiana. Creator of LIHTC Explained.

I'm a real estate developer in Shreveport, Louisiana. My day job is putting affordable housing deals together: Low-Income Housing Tax Credits (9% and 4% bond deals), RAD conversions, and layering the financing it takes to get apartments built.

I came to development through finance. I spent nearly a decade in the credit union industry, most recently as CFO. Along the way I've also started and run a few small businesses. That shapes how I look at a deal: start with the numbers, then figure out what it takes to make them work.

On the side I run LIHTC Explained, a short-video series on how affordable housing actually gets financed and built: tax credits, capital stacks, QAPs, RAD and the rules behind them. Most people never see how these deals come together, so I explain it in plain English. It's education only, my own views, and never about specific deals I work on.

M.B.A., Centenary College of Louisiana. B.S. in Finance, Louisiana Tech University.
