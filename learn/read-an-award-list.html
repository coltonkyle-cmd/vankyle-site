How to read an award list

Every state posts who applied and who won.

The course / Module 3: Winning the award / Lesson 3.4

Last reviewed November 2026

Every state posts who applied and who won. It's public, and it's one of the best ways to learn the business.

  1. Find the list. Search your state housing finance agency's site for "housing tax credit awards" or "application log."
  2. Check the set-aside or region. Most states award by region and by pool (nonprofit, rural, elderly, at-risk). Applications only compete inside their pool.
  3. Look at the scores. Winners are often separated by a point or two, and ties are broken by rules written in the QAP.
  4. Divide credits by units. Annual credits per unit shows how much each deal needed, and how far over the state's limits it was pushing.
  5. Watch the waitlist. Returned credits go to the next deal in line, sometimes months later.
Key points
  • Award lists are public.
  • Deals compete within regions and pools.
  • Scores are often one or two points apart.
  • Credits per unit is the quickest comparison.
Terms in this lesson
  • QAP (Qualified Allocation Plan)
Sources
  • State housing finance agency award and application logs; formats vary by state.
