Module 5 · Lesson 5.6

Senior vs. family deals

Many tax credit properties are age-restricted.

The course / Module 5: Living there / Lesson 5.6

Many tax credit properties are age-restricted. Under fair housing law, there are two ways to do it:

  • 62 and older: every resident is 62+.
  • 55 and older: at least 80% of occupied units have at least one resident who's 55+, and the property follows policies showing it's meant for older adults.

They pencil differently than family deals. Senior deals are mostly one-bedrooms, so rent per unit is lower. They usually need elevators, more common space and more accessible units. Some states reserve credits or points specifically for elderly housing.

Family deals have more two- and three-bedroom units and higher rents per unit, but more wear, more turnover and different amenities, like playgrounds.

Key points
  • Two legal options: 62+ or 55+.
  • Senior deals skew to one-bedrooms.
  • Elevators and common space add cost.
  • Some QAPs set aside credits for seniors.
