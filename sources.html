Sources and updates

Where the numbers and rules on this site come from, and what's changed recently.

What changed

  • Nov. 2026: IRS released 2027 amounts (Rev. Proc. 2026-35): housing credit ceiling of $3.53 per resident or $4,082,400 minimum; private activity bond cap of $135 per resident or $410,520,000 minimum; rehab minimum of $9,000 per low-income unit.
  • Oct. 5, 2026: HUD announced the 2027 Qualified Census Tracts and Difficult Development Areas (basis boost map).
  • May 12, 2026: HUD began winding down RAD Restore-Rebuild (formerly Faircloth-to-RAD) for new requests.
  • May 1, 2026: HUD's FY2026 income limits took effect, with a 45-day grace period. Increases were capped at 10%; the average change was about 3.4%.
  • Jan. 1, 2026: The permanent 12% increase in 9% credit allocations began ($3.416 per resident for 2026), and the 25% bond test began for bonds issued after 2025.
  • July 4, 2025: Public Law 119-21 was signed. A proposed 30% basis boost for rural and tribal areas was not included in the final law.
