Sources and updates

Where the numbers and rules on this site come from, and what's changed recently.

Last reviewed November 2026

What changed

  • Nov. 24, 2026: New home: this site is now lihtcexplained.com. Old vankyleproperties.com links forward here. Social handles are now @lihtcexplained. LIHTC Explained is a VanKyle Properties publication.
  • Nov. 23, 2026: New page: income and rent limits in your county, a quick lookup using HUD's FY2026 MTSP limits.
  • Nov. 23, 2026: Module 2 (Sizing the credit) lessons expanded to full depth: worked examples, the first-year credit rule, how QCT/DDA maps are drawn, a sorted sample budget, a sample equity pay-in schedule, nonprofit qualification, and how credit and bond caps are built and handed out.
  • Nov. 2026: IRS released 2027 amounts (Rev. Proc. 2026-35): housing credit ceiling of $3.53 per resident or $4,082,400 minimum; private activity bond cap of $135 per resident or $410,520,000 minimum; rehab minimum of $9,000 per low-income unit.
  • Oct. 5, 2026: HUD announced the 2027 Qualified Census Tracts and Difficult Development Areas (basis boost map).
  • May 12, 2026: HUD began winding down RAD Restore-Rebuild (formerly Faircloth-to-RAD) for new requests.
  • May 1, 2026: HUD's FY2026 income limits took effect, with a 45-day grace period. Increases were capped at 10%; the average change was about 3.4%.
  • Jan. 1, 2026: The permanent 12% increase in 9% credit allocations began ($3.416 per resident for 2026). The 25% bond test began for buildings placed in service after 2025 with at least 5% of building and land cost financed by bonds issued after 2025.
  • July 4, 2025: Public Law 119-21 was signed. A proposed 30% basis boost for rural and tribal areas was not included in the final law.
