Income and rent limits in your county

Pick your state and county to see the income limits and maximum rents for tax credit apartments there.

How to read it

  • Find your household size. Count everyone who will live in the apartment. The limit goes up as the household gets bigger.
  • Look at the 60% and 50% rows first. Most tax credit units are for households at or below 60% of the area median income, and some are set lower. A few properties also have units at 80%.
  • Rent is capped by the apartment, not your paycheck. The maximum rent for each unit size includes an allowance for utilities you pay, so the rent you're quoted is often lower than the cap. Why rent is lower than the cap →

The numbers come from HUD, which publishes them every year. The official source is HUD's MTSP income limits page. If a property's numbers look different, go by the property and your state housing finance agency.
