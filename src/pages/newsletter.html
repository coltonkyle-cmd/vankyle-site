LIHTC Explained

The free newsletter

Every other Tuesday. How affordable housing actually gets built, in plain English.

What you get

  • One idea, explained. How a piece of the tax credit world works, the way a developer would explain it across the desk.
  • One number that matters. A rent cap, a credit price, a deadline, with where it comes from.
  • What changed. New HUD and IRS numbers, rule changes, and new lessons on this site.
  • A reader question. Send yours and it might be the next one.

Who it's for

People who work in affordable housing or want to: developers, lenders, syndicators and investors, housing authority and agency staff, consultants, and anyone getting into the business. If you rent a tax credit apartment and want to know how it works, you're welcome here too.
