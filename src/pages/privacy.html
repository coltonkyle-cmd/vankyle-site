Privacy

Privacy policy

Short version: this site doesn't sell anything, doesn't run ads, and only collects what it needs to send the newsletter and count visits.

Last updated November 25, 2026.

LIHTC Explained (www.lihtcexplained.com) is a free education site published by VanKyle Properties, LLC. This page explains what information the site and its social accounts handle and what happens to it.

Visiting the site

The site counts visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics tool. It keeps totals, like how many people viewed a page or which site sent them, and doesn't store your IP address or keep a record of you as a person. The site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Some tools on the site remember choices in your own browser (for example, the state you picked in a lookup, or that you closed the newsletter box). That stays on your device and isn't sent to us. You can clear it anytime by clearing your browser's site data.

The newsletter

If you sign up for the LIHTC Explained newsletter, your email address goes to Kit, the email service that sends it. We use it only to send the newsletter. We don't sell or share your email. Every email has an unsubscribe link, or you can email us to be removed.

If you email us

If you write to colton@vankyleproperties.com, we use what you send only to reply to you.

Google and YouTube data

LIHTC Explained has a small private tool that uses the YouTube API Services to read and reply to comments on our own YouTube channel and to manage our own videos. It signs in only to our channel's Google account. It doesn't ask other people to sign in and doesn't access anyone else's Google account. Public comments left on our videos are read so we can reply to them, and aren't sold or shared.

Because of this, YouTube's Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy also apply. You can review or remove an app's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Links to other sites

Lessons link to outside sources like HUD, the IRS and state agencies. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Questions

Email colton@vankyleproperties.com. If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
