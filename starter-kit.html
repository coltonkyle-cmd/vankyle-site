The deal timeline, at a glance

  1. Find it and control it. A site that scores under your state's QAP, plus an option or purchase contract.
  2. Apply. Market study, environmental report, lender and investor letters, then the state's round (9%) or a bond request (4%).
  3. Win it and lock it in. 9%: a reservation, then a carryover allocation, with more than 10% of expected basis spent within 12 months. 4%: a public (TEFRA) hearing and the state's feasibility sign-off.
  4. Close. Construction loan, equity, soft loans and (for 4%) bonds. Record the extended use agreement.
  5. Build. Monthly draws and inspections.
  6. Place in service and lease up. 9% carryover deals: by December 31 of the second year after the allocation year.
  7. Get the 8609s. Costs are certified and the state issues Form 8609 for each building. The 10-year credit period runs from placed in service (or the next year, by election).
