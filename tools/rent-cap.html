How it works: each apartment size is priced off 1.5 people per bedroom (1 person for a studio). HUD scales the 4-person limit by household size: 70% for 1 person, 80% for 2, 90% for 3, 108% for 5, 116% for 6. Maximum gross rent is 30% of that limit divided by 12, and the utility allowance comes out of it. More in who can live there and the utility allowance.

Education only. HUD's published limits include rounding and adjustments for high- and low-cost areas, so they won't exactly match a straight percentage of median income. For real numbers, use HUD's published MTSP limits for your county and type the 4-person figure in above. Example utility allowances are made up.
